The link. A communication-engineering spine for space and non-terrestrial radio — and a working answer to the question of what a textbook leaves out when the base station is moving at seven and a half kilometres a second.

Pricing a build

A bill of materials is a CSV; ertabat bom prices it against distributor APIs — Nexar, Mouser, element14, DigiKey — or against a local price book when you would rather not hold an account anywhere.

ertabat bom bom/cubesat-1u-lband.csv --currency INR --out-md BOM.md

Keys are paths to files, never flags. A line nobody could price stays unpriced and blocks the combined total, because a total over the priced half of a bill of materials reads as the cost of the build. A bill quoted in two currencies has no total until you supply an exchange rate that names its source and its date.

What is deliberately missing

  • The ITU-R rain and gaseous-absorption tables are empty, so every Ku and Ka figure comes back as an unknown with the recommendation number attached.
  • No radio driver is bound; every device is a stub that refuses to produce samples rather than returning zeros that look like a quiet band.
  • The five state-of-the-art tracks sit at SPEC — neuromorphic on-board processing, optical inter-satellite links, cell-free MIMO across a swarm, semantic transmission, and quantum key distribution. Promotion needs evidence in the tree, and the tool refuses without it.

Each absence carries a work packet with inputs, an interface and the command that decides whether the work is finished:

ertabat doctor      # what is absent right now
ertabat dispatch    # the packets that fill it
